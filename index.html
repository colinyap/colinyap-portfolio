About. Who I am, and how I ended up pointed at chip design.

I'm a Year 2 MEng (Hons) student at the University of Nottingham Malaysia, and I've spent it building across as much of engineering as I can reach — mechanical assemblies, embedded firmware, control systems, computer vision, and digital logic. The common thread isn't a single field; it's that I like taking something from a rough idea to a thing that actually works.

That's meant a PID-controlled line-following robot with computer vision, a wireless robotic hand driven by a flex-sensor glove, and a business card etched onto a circuit board. Each one pulled me deeper into a different discipline — motor control, wireless comms, mechanical design, board layout.

Digital design is where that's settled. I've built a RISC-V processor in Verilog, added two instruction set extensions to it, and pushed it all the way through to a manufacturable chip layout using the open-source OpenLane flow. My aim is RTL design and design verification in Malaysia's semiconductor industry.
